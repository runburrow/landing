You've been invited to Burrow.

Someone who uses Burrow thinks you'd like it too. It catches your app's errors and groups them into issues, with stack traces and breadcrumbs, and it works with the Sentry SDK you may already use.

  • Free, and yours. Your own Burrow at its own address. Nobody else can sign in to it, us included.
  • Ready in a minute. Pick an address and we email you a link to set your password.
  • Move out whenever. It's open source: run it yourself later and keep your SDK setup.
